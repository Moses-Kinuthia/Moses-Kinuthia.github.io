About Me

I am a cybersecurity professional in active transition, building toward a remote Tier 1 SOC Analyst role at an MSSP or remote-first security team. My foundation is practical — I have designed, built, and operate a distributed home lab that mirrors real enterprise security architecture.

My background at KCB Bank in fraud detection, KYC, and AML compliance is not incidental — it is a strategic differentiator.
